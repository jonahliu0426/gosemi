Beyond the scanner — the EUV ecosystem (not in the machine BOM, but in every EUV wafer)

Resist & Track

Tokyo Electron (TYO: 8035) is the sole EUV-qualified inline coater/developer (CLEAN TRACK LITHIUS Pro Z). Chemically-amplified resists from JSR (taken private by JIC; owns Inpria metal-oxide resist), TOK (TYO: 4186), Shin-Etsu (TYO: 4063), Sumitomo Chemical, DuPont.

Mask-Making Toolchain

Multi-beam writers: IMS Nanofabrication (Intel-majority; Bain & TSMC minorities) and NuFlare (Toshiba). Actinic (at-wavelength) blank/pattern inspection: Lasertec (TYO: 6920) — near-monopoly. Mask repair: ZEISS MeRiT. E-beam review: NuFlare, KLA.

Metrology & Inspection

Overlay/CD feedback loops via ASML YieldStar, KLA (KLAC) optical inspection, Applied Materials (AMAT) & Hitachi High-Tech CD-SEM/e-beam. Computational loop pulls through ASML software attach revenue per scanner.

Service & Upgrades

EUV availability contracts, collector swaps, source-power and throughput upgrade packages. ASML Installed Base Management ≈ ~25% of revenue and rising with the EUV fleet — the recurring-revenue kicker on every system below.
